Student wellness

General well-being resources
🌬️

One-minute breathing

Relax your shoulders. Breathe in gently for 4 counts and out for 4 counts, without forcing it. Repeat comfortably for one minute.

👀

Screen break

Look away from your screen at a distant object, blink normally, stand up, and stretch gently.

🌙

Rest supports learning

Keep a consistent sleep routine where possible. Rest, meals, movement, and downtime belong in a sustainable study plan.

Stysa AI provides general educational wellness information, not medical diagnosis or treatment. If you feel unsafe or overwhelmed, contact a trusted adult or qualified professional.
